    🧩 Text sections and features

    Standard: ELA.6.R.2.1 · Explain how sections and features convey meaning

    Every part has a job

    To see how a part adds meaning, ask: “What does this add to the big idea?”

    • 🏷️ Subheadings name the topic of a section.
    • 📊 Diagrams and labels show what the words describe.
    • ⏳ Timelines show events in order or how things change.
    • 📦 Sidebars and glossaries explain terms or add related facts.
    • 🔤 Bold words tell you a key term is being defined.

    Sections also work together. One section can set up the problem, and the next can show the result.

    🌐 Research a question

    Standard: ELA.6.C.4.1 · Research using multiple reliable and valid sources, and refocus when needed

    Be a research detective

    1️⃣ Ask a focused questionNot too big, not yes/no. “How do service dogs help people?” works better than “Dogs?”
    2️⃣ Use more than one sourceCompare at least two. Do they agree?
    3️⃣ Check if it’s reliableWho wrote it? Are they an expert? Is it up to date? Does it list sources? Is it trying to teach you or sell you something?
    4️⃣ RefocusToo many results? Make your question narrower. Too few? Try new key words or a bigger idea.
